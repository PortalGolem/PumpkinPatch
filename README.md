WELCOME to the Hilly Hills Pumpkin Patch. Unfortunately, due to the accident, you can no longer pick your own pumpkins.
A dedicated pumpkin fetcher will take your order and try to find the best product for you.

To incoming pumpkin fetchers. Your job is to gather pumpkins from the patch based off of people's orders.

You are to fulfill their request to the greatest extent possible, you never know what those details might contribute to their final carving.

I started this game because I wanted to make something fall related. I was at a pumpkin patch and I just liked the vibes and the way the shadows grew long early in the day.
Unfortunately development has been quite slow, especially because I've been getting back into Godot after working on another project with Unity for a while.

Currently the game is pretty limited. You can pick up items and you can pick up pumpkins and place them back down. 
There is also some axe functionality but it isn't quite finished yet.

How to play

You can move around with WASD and look with the mouse. 
You can also press left click to use your axe (although that isn't implemented yet) and right click to pick up/drop items.
